What you get

What we do not promise

The service is provided as it is, without warranty. Specifically, and stated plainly because it matters more here than in most software:

  • ICR guarantees structure, not meaning. It will not emit code that fails to parse, and it will refuse a merge rather than ship one it cannot validate. It cannot tell you that a merge which parses is the merge you wanted.
  • Coordination between agents reduces collisions; it does not eliminate the need for review. Keep your tests and keep reading the diffs.
  • An agent acts with your permissions. The GitHub watcher runs the agent you choose on your own machine, and pushes branches and opens pull requests with your GitHub login. Hivecode never merges: what it opens is yours to review before you merge it.
