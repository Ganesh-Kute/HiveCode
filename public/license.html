your licence

Check a key

Paste a licence key to see what it covers and whether it is currently active. This is the same check the relay makes before admitting a connection, so what you see here is exactly what your rooms will do.

Thirty-two characters after HC-PRO-. Nothing is stored and nothing is logged — the key is sent to the validator and the answer is shown to you.

Lost the key itself?

Your licence is stored the moment payment clears, so it is never gone. Recover it with the Payment Id from your Razorpay receipt.

Need to change your seat count?

Seats live on the record rather than in the key, so they can be adjusted without reissuing anything. Tell us the number you need and we will change it — for now this is a manual step, deliberately, while volumes are small enough that a human should look at every change.
